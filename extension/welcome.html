What leaves your computer

With Chrome's built-in translator, which Paragloss uses until you choose another one, nothing you translate leaves this computer.

If you pick an AI provider in the settings, the texts you translate or write in Paragloss, with a short context it keeps for each site, go straight to that provider with your own key, once you agree there. Paragloss has no server and no analytics. Read the privacy policy.
